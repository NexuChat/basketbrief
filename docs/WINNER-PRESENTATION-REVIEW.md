## Changes to BasketBrief

- Start with the consequence: a report has been sent, then the contributor corrects its count.
- Present one complete causal sequence: evidence gap → direct question → source review → human approval → donor copy → late correction → fresh approval → amendment.
- Record the actual contributor inboxes, source dialog and donor views, not just the coordinator dashboard.
- Crop to the UI element being discussed. Keep a short editorial heading beside it. Preserve the original UI pixels and values.
- Use distinct narrative chapters and a brief visual transition at the late correction. Keep architecture after the demonstrated result.
- Use an original, quiet generated musical underscore; intelligible narration comes first.
- Keep disclosures legible. Fictional inputs, staging accounts and real inference are separate facts.
- Use no field-pilot, customer-endorsement or time-saving claim. The owner reported no available organization tester.

The current competition's requirements remain authoritative: [Agents for Humans rules](https://agentsforhumans.devpost.com/rules). Its video maximum is five minutes; its criteria weight presentation equally with implementation, design, impact and originality. A winning video from a different competition does not establish the right duration or scoring for this one.

A direct yt-dlp download was also attempted for Jayu and EcoLafaek, including with an explicit Node JavaScript runtime. YouTube required sign-in to confirm the client was not a bot. Signing in may resolve that gate; no successful YouTube download or complete EcoLafaek video review is claimed.
