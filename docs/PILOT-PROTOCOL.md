# First user validation — prepared, not performed

No relief coordinator or organization has tested BasketBrief in a field pilot. The owner confirmed that no organization tester is currently available. A private Gmail import test establishes an integration result, not organization demand or time saved.

## Small, measurable trial

Recruit a coordinator who actually handles volunteer evidence and donor reporting. Obtain permission before contacting them, recording them or attributing feedback. Use synthetic source documents initially; do not collect beneficiary records.

Give the participant two comparable source packets with a missing receipt and a late field correction. Counterbalance the order across participants: one packet with their normal tools and the other with BasketBrief. Include time spent learning the tool, reading sources, answering questions and reviewing results. Report setup time separately, without removing it from the total.

Ask them to:

1. Prepare consistent reports for two donors.
2. Resolve a missing receipt without counting the expense twice.
3. Handle a corrected delivery count after the first report was sent.
4. Explain which version each donor has and what is still unknown.

Record task completion, elapsed human time, incorrect or unsupported figures, missed recipients, interventions by the facilitator, and whether the participant could identify the original source and approval history. Preserve failures and incomplete attempts.

Use these questions after the task:

- What did you expect BasketBrief to do that it did not do?
- Where did you need to double-check it?
- Which step, if any, would you use in your real workflow?
- What would prevent your team from using it?

Publish only consented, anonymized findings with sample size, task conditions and observed limitations. Do not extrapolate a small study into hours or money saved across organizations.
